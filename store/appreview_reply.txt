Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing our app. Please find the requested information below. A screen recording captured on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS is attached.

1. Screen recording
The attached recording starts from launching the app and shows the typical user flow: the tracking explanation and App Tracking Transparency prompt, answering a "Past Questions" quiz (choosing up/down, viewing the answer and the following 5 business days), the title/points bar increasing, the "Today's Prediction" tab (submitting a vote and the result notification permission), the "History" tab with filters and notes, and the Settings screen (chart days, transfer code).
The app has no account registration, no login, no user-generated content shared with other users, and no paid content or In-App Purchases.

2. Purpose and target audience
"FX日足過去問" (FX Daily Chart Quiz) is a practice app for people in Japan who trade or want to learn foreign exchange (FX). Users look at a real historical daily exchange-rate chart and guess whether the rate goes up or down on the next business day. The answer is shown immediately, together with the movement of the following 5 business days. Users can also make a daily prediction for the next business day, which is judged automatically when the new reference rate is published. The app helps users practice reading charts without risking money. It has no trading function and does not provide investment advice.

3. How to use the app
No login or sample files are required. After launch, the "過去問" (Past Questions) tab opens. Tap "上がる" (Up) or "下がる" (Down), then "回答する" (Answer) to see the result. Tap "次の問題へ" (Next) for a new question. The "今日の予想" (Today's Prediction) tab lets users vote on the next business day. The "記録" (History) tab shows past answers and notes. Settings (gear icon, top right) let users change the number of chart days and export/import a transfer code for device migration.

4. External services
- European Central Bank (ECB) euro foreign exchange reference rates: the source of all exchange-rate data. The data is downloaded once per business day by our own GitHub Actions job and served as a static JSON file on GitHub Pages (https://studio-akari.github.io/fx-hiashi/ecb.json). The app only downloads this file. Historical data is also bundled in the app.
- Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK and the User Messaging Platform): displays banner and interstitial ads and handles ad consent.
- Apple local notifications: notify the user when the daily prediction result is available. No push server is used.
All user records and notes are stored only on the device and are never sent to any server. The app uses no AI services, no authentication services and no payment processors.

5. Regional differences
The app is distributed only in Japan. The app works the same for all users; there are no regional differences in features or content.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material
The app is not a financial service. It does not offer trading, brokerage, or investment advice, and this is stated in the app and in the description. The exchange rates are the ECB's public euro reference rates. The ECB permits free reuse of this information provided the source is cited; the app cites "European Central Bank (ECB)" as the source on every screen and in Settings. Rates for non-euro pairs (e.g. USD/JPY) are calculated from the ECB rates, and this is also stated. ECB copyright and reuse policy: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/disclaimer/html/index.en.html

Thank you,
studio Akari
